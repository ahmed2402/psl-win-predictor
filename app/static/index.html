        How it works

        A logistic regression trained on every completed, full-length PSL chase from 2015/16 to 2025 (Cricsheet ball-by-ball data), one row per ball. It reads only the live state: balls left, runs needed, wickets, required and current run rate, runs and wickets in the last 12 balls, plus today's evening dew point for the city from Open-Meteo.

        Team names are labels only. Venue, toss and target were tested and left out: their effect already shows up in the live state, and venue behaviour changed between eras.

        Recent seasons score higher than the training years, so the predictor can underrate chasers in the middle range. Early in a chase it is honestly uncertain.
