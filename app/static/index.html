        How it works

        Logistic regression on every full-length PSL chase, 2015/16 to 2025, ball by ball (Cricsheet). Inputs: the live match state plus tonight's dew point for the city (Open-Meteo).

        Teams, venue and toss are not inputs: their effect already shows in the live state.

        Recent high-scoring seasons can make it underrate chasers mid-innings.

        Unofficial fan project, not affiliated with the PSL or its teams. Team badges are original designs.
